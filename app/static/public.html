SEO Audit Agent

Paste a website. Get the full audit.

It crawls the site, checks technical SEO, titles and meta, headings, images, schema, broken links, duplicate content, AI-search visibility, page speed and competitors — then hands back a report you can download and open in any browser.

Takes about 60–120 seconds. Nothing is stored on the server.

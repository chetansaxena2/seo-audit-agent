How much does an outstation cab cost?

Outstation cabs start at eleven rupees per kilometre with a two hundred and fifty kilometre minimum on one way routes. Round trips are billed at a lower rate and include a night charge only when the driver stays overnight.

Which cities do you cover from Delhi?

We run daily trips to Jaipur, Agra, Chandigarh, Dehradun, Rishikesh, Haridwar, Shimla and Manali, along with any other destination within a twelve hour drive of Delhi NCR.

Do you provide airport pickups?

Yes. Airport pickups include flight tracking and sixty minutes of free waiting time, so a delayed landing never costs you extra money.
